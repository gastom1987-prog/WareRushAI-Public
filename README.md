# WareRushAI

**One inventory workflow for overstock, returns, and recommerce.**

WareRushAI is built for merchants who need to receive products, prepare listings, manage stock, and sell across more than one channel. Its aim is to bring the webshop, marketplaces, invoicing, and in-store work together around shared product and inventory information.

AI assists with product identification and channel-specific product data. People remain involved in decisions that need merchant review.

## The problem WareRushAI addresses

Overstock and returned-goods operations often involve product details, photos, barcodes, marketplace requirements, stock, and orders spread across separate tools. Each marketplace can require different categories, attributes, product-safety information, condition details, and shipping settings. Missing or invalid information can prevent a listing from being published.

WareRushAI aims to make this work easier to manage in one workflow: capture and prepare products, review the data, manage inventory, and connect selected channels.

## What it does

- Captures products by barcode or photo
- Helps prepare product information and marketplace-specific fields
- Suggests product identification, categories, and pricing with AI assistance
- Checks product data against channel requirements and explains missing information
- Supports inventory, orders, and product synchronization across connected channels
- Connects in-store and warehouse workflows with online sales operations

Availability and exact capabilities can vary by integration. See the [integration overview](docs/integrations.md) and the official [integrations page](https://warerushai.com/integrations) for current details.
