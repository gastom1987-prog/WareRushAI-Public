# WareRushAI features

This page summarizes merchant-facing workflows described on the official WareRushAI website. Exact availability and behavior may depend on the connected channel. See https://warerushai.com/features and https://warerushai.com/integrations for current product information.

## Product intake and preparation

WareRushAI is designed to help merchants bring product intake and inventory work into a shared workflow.

- Capture products by barcode or photo.
- Prepare product information for resale.
- Use AI assistance for product identification and suggestions.
- Suggest categories and pricing based on available product information.
- Review and approve important decisions as a merchant.

## Marketplace listing preparation

The website describes a workflow intended to help merchants meet different marketplace requirements:

- Fill channel-specific required fields using available product information and photos.
- Select a category and prepare required attributes for each enabled marketplace.
- Check products against channel rules before submission.
- Keep incomplete products from being submitted as if they were ready.
- Explain in plain language what merchant-supplied information is missing, such as a barcode from the product packaging.
- Use rejection feedback to inform subsequent data preparation and corrections.

These descriptions refer to the product workflow; specific behavior depends on the marketplace and the data available for a product. AI suggestions are not a substitute for merchant review or for facts that must be supplied by the merchant.

## Inventory and sales channels

The website describes connected channels using shared inventory and product data, with product, price, and stock updates. Depending on the integration, order information can also flow into WareRushAI's order and warehouse workflows.

## Store and warehouse operations

The website describes tablet-based point-of-sale workflows, barcode-based warehouse work from a phone, and product or shelf label printing with Brother and other label printers.

## Invoicing

The website describes invoice creation from store, partner, and marketplace orders through connected invoicing services, including document PDF and cancellation workflows where supported by the service.

## AI-assisted operations

The website describes connecting ChatGPT and Claude to WareRushAI data and operations with user permission and secure sign-in. The exact actions and permissions available should be checked in the product and current integration documentation.

## Human review

AI assists with identification and suggestions. Merchants remain responsible for reviewing information and approving important decisions, especially where physical product attributes or compliance information must be confirmed.
