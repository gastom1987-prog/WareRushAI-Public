# WareRushAI FAQ

## What is WareRushAI?

WareRushAI is an inventory and sales-channel workflow for merchants who handle overstock, returned goods, and recommerce products. It aims to bring product intake, product data preparation, stock management, and connected sales workflows together.

## Who is WareRushAI for?

It is designed for small businesses and merchant teams that process overstock or returned goods and sell products through webshops, marketplaces, or physical stores.

## How does WareRushAI use AI?

AI assists with product identification and suggestions, including preparation of product information for sales channels. Merchants review and approve important decisions and supply details that need physical verification.

## Which marketplaces are listed?

The official integrations page lists Allegro, eMAG, Amazon, eBay, Alza, Temu, and Pepita. See https://warerushai.com/integrations for the current service-specific descriptions.

## Which webshops are listed?

The official integrations page lists Shopify, WooCommerce, UNAS, and Shoprenter. See https://warerushai.com/integrations for the current details.

## Which price-comparison services are listed?

The official page lists Árukereső, Árgép, and Google Shopping.

## Which invoicing services are listed?

The official page lists Számlázz.hu and Billingo.

## Can WareRushAI support store and warehouse operations?

The official page describes a tablet-based POS, SAM4S online cash-register integration, label printing, and barcode-based mobile warehouse work. Refer to the product website for current details.

## Can I connect ChatGPT or Claude?

The integrations page describes a WareRushAI connector for ChatGPT and Claude, subject to secure sign-in and user permission. Check the official page for current details and supported actions.

## Can I request another integration?

The official site invites requests for systems with an official API, including ERP systems, local carriers, and custom webshops. Contact api@warerushai.com.
