# WareRushAI usage guide (overview)

This is a short map of the workflow. The step-by-step guide for every screen is on the website and is kept current there: https://warerushai.com/guide

## The workflow

1. **Start**: register, create or join a company workspace, set company details and warehouse locations. See [Register and create your company](https://warerushai.com/guide/getting-started).
2. **Capture products**: scan a barcode or take a photo; the basic warehouse record is saved first and background work prepares product data. See [Your first product from a photo](https://warerushai.com/guide/first-product).
3. **Stock and shelves**: manage inventory per location, shelf positions and stock movements. See [Inventory and shelf locations](https://warerushai.com/guide/inventory-shelves).
4. **Connect channels**: webshops (see [Connect Shopify](https://warerushai.com/guide/shopify) and [Connect WooCommerce](https://warerushai.com/guide/woocommerce)), marketplaces and invoicing. Status per service: [integrations](https://warerushai.com/integrations).
5. **Orders**: pick orders in shelf order and issue documents. See [Prepare an order in shelf order](https://warerushai.com/guide/picking) and [Sales and returns](https://warerushai.com/guide/sales-returns).
6. **Catalogue, partners and B2B**: see [Catalogue, partners, cart and orders](https://warerushai.com/guide/catalogue-orders).
7. **Settings and plan**: [Settings and company defaults](https://warerushai.com/guide/settings) and [Trial and subscription](https://warerushai.com/guide/subscription).

## When something fails

Known errors, their cause, what the system does by itself and what you need to do are documented per channel, for example [Allegro](https://warerushai.com/guide/troubleshooting-allegro). The guide index lists all `troubleshooting-` chapters.

## Use your own AI assistant

You can connect Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP-capable assistant (read-only) and ask about your stock, products, listing problems and open orders: [MCP connector guide](mcp-connector.md).

## Notes

AI assists with product identification and suggestions; people review important decisions. The website is the source of truth for current availability and capabilities.
